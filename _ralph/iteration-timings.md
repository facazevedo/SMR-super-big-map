# T0-to-T1 optimization iteration timings

This is the append-only timing ledger for the Surface T0-to-T1 optimization loop.
Update it at the end of every iteration, including rejected and interrupted iterations.

Timing rules:

- **Cycle time** runs from selecting one optimization through its final verdict, including implementation, focused validation, review, deployment, live testing, and diagnosis.
- **T0-to-T1** is the authoritative in-harness Stopwatch measurement when one is produced. Clearly label process/Lua estimates as non-authoritative.
- **Overhead** is cycle time minus T0-to-T1. The operational target is at most **600 seconds total per cycle**.
- **Accepted best** changes whenever the complete acceptance contract passes and the candidate is faster than the prior accepted best.
- **Goal met** is a separate condition: the current optimization goal is strictly below **60 seconds**. A correct improvement does not have to reach the final goal to become the next baseline.
